      How the routes are found
      • Network model: stations are vertices; each track between two neighbouring stations is an edge with its minutes. A station on two lines is an interchange.
      • Fastest and Fewest Interchanges: Dijkstra's algorithm with a binary min-heap. Each priority compares time, stops, transfers and walking in a different order, so ties are broken in a sensible way.
      • Shortest means the fewest stops (or the least distance, once real distances are added to the data file).
      • Alternatives: a depth-first search lists every real route that visits no station twice; very slow ones are hidden.
      • Not available means the data was not provided. Nothing is guessed.
